Our story

It was 2020. The world slowed down, everyone was stuck at home, and suddenly there was time to do things you’d normally never get around to. So she started a bucket list.

One night at a friend’s house, she overheard him talking about making a quick fishing run later that night. Her ears perked up immediately.

“Fishing!? That’s on my bucket list. Can I come?”Liliana, 2020

His eyes lit up like a deer in headlights… and of course he said yes.

So that night they went fishing. She casually baited her own hook (which may have been the exact moment he fell in love), and he somehow managed to catch a lobster. Not bad for a random late-night outing. (Though her mom may not have thought so, since she got home at 3 AM…)

Somewhere between the fishing, the laughs, the squid and the lobster, something just clicked. They’ve been inseparable ever since.
